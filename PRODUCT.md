# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Stack

Native Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3 structure. Design is prototyped as a Design canvas in `docs/canvas/project/`. Full plan in `PLAN.md`.

## Users

Tamil and English readers, in India, Sri Lanka and the diaspora, who want a calm daily writing habit, not another feed. They read Tamil; many type it through Gboard. They open the app once a day, often in the morning or late at night, alone, phone in hand.

## Product Purpose

Each day opens with one Thirukkural couplet, a plain meaning and a question to write from. The user writes a private page. Success is a steady, guilt-free habit and a journal the user trusts with their real thoughts.

## Positioning

The only journal built on the Thirukkural, with Valluvar himself as a warm companion, and the only one that provably cannot go online: it has no internet permission at all.

## Operating Context

Daily ritual: read the couplet, write, save. Moments that matter: the first launch, the save, a new seal, the streak (called the thread), looking back on this day a year ago, and writing in Tamil. Backups go to Google Drive through Android's file picker. Home-screen widgets show the day's kural.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No INTERNET permission. No accounts, ads, analytics or AI at runtime.
- Couplets always set on exactly two lines, sized to fit, in Anek Tamil condensed.
- 1,330 kurals; 120 prompts at launch, 365 later. Tamil kural text only from the source file.
- Streak is "the thread", with two rest days a week kept automatically, never shown as a loss. Nine seals with Tamil numerals.
- Android 8.0 and up, phones first, Google Play first.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Kural Heart" in Latin letters everywhere, never translated. Tagline: Journal with Valluvar / குறளுடன் நாட்குறிப்பு.
- Valluvar as the companion: a bold, rounded character in the spirit of Duolingo characters, the owner's own model (AnimatedThiruvalluvar, from their animation studio source), in ten states with natural, fluid motion, white veshti and shawl with a gold border, no holy ash, no saffron, no religious mark, no halo. Never frowns or scolds. Never on the couplet leaf itself, a Page or a notification; on Write only as a small scribe in the toolbar.
- Logo: the heart-beard mark, Valluvar's bust on a dawn tile, his beard shaped as the heart with a smile cut out.
- Concepts the owner has liked: the palm leaf (ஏடு), the thread (நூல்), the six parts of the day (சிறுபொழுது).
- The owner wants it to look and feel premium. Offered a complete rethink on 8 Nov 2026 (cinema banners, a tear-off calendar, the category standard), the owner set the rule instead: **a journal app should look like a journal, the Thirukkural is the main idea, Tamil aesthetics are better but must not be overdone.** Palette B, Leaf and lamp, refined into a journal, was the world until 9 Nov 2026, when the owner rejected the whole look and asked for a complete, industry-standard rework. **The world is now v6, "Olai": an editorial journal on warm ivory (Midnight at night), serif headings, one clay accent, rounded cards, the drawn palm leaf as hero, and the owner's own Valluvar model in ten states.** The owner's own showcases (warm editorial, terracotta, flat Valluvar in white) are the reference.
- Part of Heart Apps, with the Heart Apps chip and card on About.

## Product Principles

1. Calm first: one main action per screen.
2. Tamil is first class.
3. Provably private.
4. Reward, never punish.
5. Respect the text: couplets exactly as sourced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Font scale to 200 percent with no clipped Tamil; TalkBack reads a couplet as one unit; contrast 4.5 to 1 for text, measured; full reduced-motion path; Tamil and English strings.
