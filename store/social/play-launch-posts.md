## LinkedIn

Kural Heart is now on Google Play.

It's a private journal with Thiruvalluvar. Each day opens with one couplet of the Thirukkural on its palm leaf, in Tamil and English, and a question to write about. You write a line or a page, and it stays on your phone.

- All 1,330 couplets, with G. U. Pope, V. V. S. Aiyar and Mu. Varadarasanar's Tamil explanation
- A pronunciation line under every couplet, for readers new to Tamil
- A question for the mind, for speech and for action
- Feelings, photos, voice notes and places on your pages
- A thread, not a streak: two rest days a week are kept for you

No account, no ads, no tracking. The journal only leaves your phone locked with your own passphrase, and Google Drive sync is off until you turn it on.

Get it here: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.inigocode.kuralheart

I'd love to hear which couplet you'd want on your first morning.

## Instagram story

1. Upload `play-launch-story.png`. The top and bottom 250 px are left clear for Instagram's own bars.
